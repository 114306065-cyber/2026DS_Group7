About MovieFinder

MovieFinder is a theme-based movie search engine.

How search and ranking work

MovieFinder re-ranks the websites with its own keyword scoring.

  1. Step Details

WebPage.score = Σ count(ki) × weight(ki)

WebNode.nodeScore = webPage.score + 0.5 × Σ child.nodeScore

WebTree.totalScore = root.nodeScore

Data source

Search results come from the Tavily Search API.
