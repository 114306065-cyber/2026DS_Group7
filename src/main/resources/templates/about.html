About MovieFinder

MovieFinder is a theme-based movie search engine.

How ranking works

  1. Step

WebPage.score = Σ count(ki) × weight(ki)

WebNode.nodeScore = webPage.score + 0.5 × Σ child.nodeScore

WebTree.totalScore = root.nodeScore
